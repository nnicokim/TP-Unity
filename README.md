# Hospital Nightmare

You wake up inside an abandoned hospital. The lights flicker, the hallways seem endless, and something is moving in the dark. You do not know how long you have been unconscious, but you know one thing for sure: you have to get out alive.

**Hospital Nightmare** is a first-person zombie shooter set in a decaying medical facility overrun by hostile creatures, buried secrets, and a final threat standing between you and the only way out.

Explore dark rooms, find weapons, manage your ammunition, and fight through waves of enemies as you search for a way to escape.

## Survive With Whatever You Find

Every weapon changes the way you play:

- **Fists** for desperate moments when there is no other choice.
- **Pistol** to survive your first encounters.
- **Rifle** to push forward when the situation escalates.
- **Shotgun** for the moments when everything gets too close.

But be careful: ammunition is limited. Every shot matters.

## A Hospital That Does Not Want You To Leave

Your goal is not just to kill zombies. Something much worse is locked inside the building. A boss enemy controls access to the exit, and defeating it is the only way to unlock your path to freedom.

As you move deeper into the hospital, you will find clues, messages, hidden weapons, and special objects that can change the flow of combat, including temporary damage boosts that can turn an impossible fight in your favor.

## Features

- First-person shooter with a horror atmosphere.
- Abandoned hospital divided into multiple levels.
- Combat against zombies and special enemies.
- Weapon system with fists, pistol, rifle, and shotgun.
- Limited ammunition and strategic reloading.
- Visual recoil, muzzle flashes, and weapon effects.
- Weapon pickups hidden throughout the environment.
- Temporary x2 damage bonus from destroying special objects.
- HUD with health, ammunition, and objective instructions.
- Boss encounter required to unlock the exit.

## Can You Escape?

Every hallway could be your last. Every bullet could save your life. Every door could hide something worse.

Enter the hospital, find the exit, and survive the nightmare.
